See the furniture in your own room

Take a photo from a doorway or corner so you can see where the back wall meets the floor at both corners. Then line up 4 dots on the floor and Fitroom places every piece at its true size.

Realistic views show each piece at its listed width and depth, in the color and finish named in the listing. Shapes are lifelike stand-ins, not scans of the exact product. 3D models: potted plant by Rico Cilliers (CC0) with materials by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group (CC BY 4.0), lounge chair by Eric Chadwick / Wayfair (CC0); textures resized for the web.
